About

Sam Marino is an aviation professional and hardware troubleshooter with a mission-first mindset and 250+ hours of GA flight experience. He maintained 5 state-of-the-art FAA-approved AATD flight simulators, including Frasca and Redbird systems, and delivered field-engineered fixes in high-volume operations.

He is strong at root-cause diagnosis and at cost-saving hardware modifications, including originating a custom breakaway gate system later adopted more broadly. He is seeking Flight Test / Mission Operations roles that combine flight experience with hands-on technical skill.

In Spring 2024 he completed Advanced Aerodynamics (flight test focus) — joint Aviation–Engineering coursework using flight-test curriculum developed by Jim Payne (Perlan Project Chairman & Chief Pilot; pilot of the hydrogen-powered Blue Condor flight-test campaign). He conducted flight-test analysis and documentation for maneuvers evaluating range and flight characteristics across airfoils, applying core test-pilot methodology.

He holds an FAA Private Pilot Certificate, an FAA Instrument Rating issued in May 2026, an FAA Remote Pilot Certificate (Part 107) since January 2025, an FAA First-Class Medical Certificate, and a Complex airplane endorsement. A B.S. in Commercial Aviation from California Baptist University is expected in December 2026. He is based in Costa Mesa, California, open to relocate, and authorized to work in the U.S. for any employer.
